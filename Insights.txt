Insight 1: Revenue grew from R$46k in Oct 2016 to R$1.15M in Nov 2017 — 25x growth in 13 months. November 2017 spike is Black Friday.

Insight 2: Bed & Bath is the top category at R$1.69M revenue across 9,272 orders.

Insight 3: São Paulo (SP) alone generates R$5.77M — nearly 3x more than second place Rio de Janeiro.

Insight 4: Average order value has been slowly declining from R$175 in 2016 to R$155 in 2018 — worth investigating.

Insight 5 — 93% of customers never repurchase. The business is entirely dependent on acquiring new customers rather than retaining existing ones. Cannot Lose segment (1,665 customers) generates R$945 avg spend but are slipping away.
